Privacy & Data

This client runs in your browser

SBCODE vLLM Client is a client-side web application. Your browser communicates directly with the vLLM endpoint that you configure.

Information stored locally

The endpoint address, API key, automatically detected model information, saved configurations, and saved conversations are stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

SBCODE does not receive your chat data

During normal operation, this client does not send your API key, saved conversations, prompts, or vLLM responses to the SBCODE web server. After the page has loaded, chat requests are sent directly from your browser to the vLLM/RunPod endpoint you configure.

RunPod / vLLM

Chat requests are processed by the vLLM service and infrastructure associated with the endpoint you configure. The infrastructure provider may process, monitor, or retain information according to its own policies, configuration, logging, and operational requirements.

Do not enter sensitive, confidential, personal, or otherwise private information into a model endpoint unless you understand and accept how that endpoint and its infrastructure handle submitted data.

Website and Cloudflare

If this client is hosted on a public website, normal website requests may involve your hosting provider and services such as Cloudflare. These services may process standard website and network information such as IP addresses, request metadata, security information, and logs.

Clearing your local data

Clearing this site's browser data will remove locally stored settings and conversations. It does not delete information that has already been sent to your configured vLLM/RunPod endpoint.
